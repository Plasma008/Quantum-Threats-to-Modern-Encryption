A beginner-friendly quantum computing project demonstrating qubits, quantum gates, circuits, and simulations using Python and Qiskit.



# Quantum Computing Project

## Overview
This project explores the fundamentals of quantum computing and demonstrates how quantum principles can be applied to solve computational problems more efficiently than classical approaches. It focuses on key concepts such as qubits, superposition, entanglement, quantum gates, and quantum circuits.

## Objectives
- Understand the core principles of quantum computing
- Study the difference between classical bits and quantum bits
- Explore quantum gates and circuit-based computation
- Analyze the role of superposition and entanglement
- Demonstrate basic quantum algorithms and their applications

## Key Concepts
- Qubits
- Superposition
- Entanglement
- Quantum Gates
- Quantum Circuits
- Quantum Measurement
- Quantum Algorithms

## Technologies Used
- Python
- Qiskit
- Jupyter Notebook
- IBM Quantum Platform

## Features
- Implementation of basic quantum circuits
- Simulation of quantum gates
- Visualization of quantum states
- Demonstration of quantum measurement outcomes
- Basic explanation of quantum algorithm workflow

## Applications
Quantum computing has potential applications in:
- Cryptography
- Optimization problems
- Drug discovery
- Artificial intelligence
- Financial modeling
- Complex simulations

## Learning Outcome
Through this project, I gained an understanding of how quantum computers process information differently from classical computers and how quantum circuits can be designed and simulated using modern quantum computing tools.
